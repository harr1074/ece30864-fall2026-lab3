a.  Pros: Allows for the reconsideration of changing consumer values, Encourages colaboration and reduced bockages through the sharing of blockers for individuals 
    Cons: Oversharing can slow down team progress, overly iterating can result in repeated work and churn

b.  Scrum Master: Helps translate the Product Owner's requirements and converts them into tasks for the team, runs sprint planning and sprint reviews.
    Product Owner: Understands business requirements, manages backlog, ensures team is focused on the right work.

c.  Sprint Planning: Each member of the team shares their tasks for the week. The Scrum master keeps the meeting on time and helps identify potential blockers
    Standup: Each member of the team shares what they did uesterday and what their plan is for the day ahead. Team members also voice blockers so others can help.
    Sprint Review: Each member of the team reviews what tasks they completed. Incomplete work is either moved to the next sprint or into the backlog

d.  Epic: Large bodies of work (sometimes requiring multiple people) that are composed of several stories
    Story: Smaller increments of work that take the form of a single requirement or customer need
    Bug: A piece of erroneous behavior to be fixed
    Task: Individual components that are required to complete a story
